SYNTHETIC EDUCATIONAL RESEARCH NOTE — NOT A CLINICAL GUIDELINE

Population-level research discussions of cardiovascular risk commonly examine dietary patterns, physical activity, sleep, tobacco avoidance, and stress management. Effects vary across people and studies. A qualified clinician should assess medical history, symptoms, medications, contraindications, and local guidelines before discussing personal care.

This demonstration note does not recommend a treatment, dose, target, or diagnosis. It exists only to test retrieval and citations in this starter project.
